Privacy & Community Ethos

Last updated October 2026.

Who can join

Common Ground is reserved strictly for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts of anyone under 18 will be removed.

What we collect

Your email, and what you choose to display on your profile: name, username, age, city, photos, worldview, interests and your answers. Messages and voice notes are stored in locked (encrypted) form.

Who sees it

Your profile and photos are visible to signed-in members. Messages and voice notes are end-to-end encrypted: locked on your device and only accessible by you and your match. Calls connect directly between devices. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Your data is safely hosted with our infrastructure provider, Supabase.

Your choices

Edit your profile any time. Deleting your account under Profile permanently erases your profile, photos, encrypted messages and voice notes.

Community ethos

Be respectful, thoughtful, and authentic. No harassment, hate speech, threats, commercial solicitations, or explicit content. Honor each person's freedom of thought and inquiry.

Safety

Block or report anyone who breaches our common ground. Reports are evaluated promptly.
