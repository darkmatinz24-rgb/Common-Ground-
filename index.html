Privacy & Rules

Last updated October 2026.

Who can join

Beyond Belief is for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts of anyone under 18 will be removed.

What we collect

Your email, and what you put on your profile: name, username, age, city, photos, worldview (a belief-related detail), interests and your answers. Messages and voice notes are stored in locked (encrypted) form, together with who sent them and when.

Who sees it

Your profile and photos are visible to signed-in members. Messages and voice notes are end-to-end encrypted: they are locked on your phone and can only be opened on yours and the other person's, so we cannot read them. If you report someone, you can choose to share recent messages with our team. Calls go directly between devices, so the other person's device may be able to see your network address (IP) during a call. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service provider, Supabase.

Your choices

Edit your profile any time. You can delete your account under My profile. This permanently removes your profile, photos, messages and voice notes.

Community rules

Be kind and honest. No harassment, hate, threats, scams or requests for money. No nudity or sexual content in photos. Use your own photos and identity. Never share another person's private details. Everyone's beliefs, and lack of belief, are respected here.

Safety

Block or report anyone who makes you uncomfortable. Reports are reviewed by our team, and accounts that break these rules may be removed. Meet new people in public places and tell a friend.
